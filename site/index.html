How it works

One loop for every table in the store. Each run adds to a history, so the checks, the thresholds and the scores get sharper the longer Sahifa watches.

01 · Profile

Read every column

Types, null shares, distinct counts, patterns, ranges and keys, computed inside your database or on the files where they lie.

02 · Check

Tests written for you

Checks are generated from the profile and its history. You lock the ones you trust, and every new suggestion waits for your approval.

03 · Score

With an error bar

A score per ISO/IEC 25012 characteristic for each column, table and store, with a 95 % interval that shows how sure the number is.

04 · Explain

Say where and why

Every finding names the rows it found, where they cluster and what to try next. Fixes are proposals you approve. Sahifa never writes to your data.

What is different

We studied the open-source and commercial tools before writing a line of code. These are the gaps Sahifa closes.

Scores you can trust

No other tool says how certain its quality score is. Sahifa follows the ISO/IEC 25024 measures and attaches an interval from the rows it read.

ISO/IEC 25012 · 25024

Every store in one pass

SQL databases, warehouses, lakehouse tables and plain files: checks run where the data lives, through one connector layer.

Postgres · Snowflake · BigQuery · DuckDB · Parquet · Iceberg · CSV

Alerts with a promise

Thresholds for volume, freshness and drift come with a false-alarm rate you choose, instead of a sensitivity slider.

Seasonal baselines · conformal thresholds

Rules for your country

Rule packs for IBANs, VAT IDs and postcodes across Europe and beyond, not only US ZIP codes and states.

ISO 13616 · EU VAT · UPU postcodes

Contracts you can take with you

A contract is inferred from each table's profile. Edit it, approve it and export it in the open standard.

Open Data Contract Standard v3

A health check for the store

Unused and duplicate tables, small-file problems on lakehouse tables and missing indexes, next to the quality findings.

Query logs · MinHash · compaction advice

Open, with nothing held back

Sahifa runs on your own server under the Apache 2.0 licence. Many users, many connections and many projects come at no cost. Your data never leaves your network, and no telemetry is sent anywhere. It is built only on engines with Apache or MIT licences, so you can embed it and host it as you like.

A sister of Tabayyun

Tabayyun verifies industrial time series. Sahifa covers the rest of the store and shares its ideas: findings that live through a lifecycle, scores per dimension, corrections as separate versions with full lineage. When Sahifa finds a timestamp column with a measured value, it hands the series to Tabayyun.

Why the name

The Ṣaḥīfa of Medina was the written charter the Prophet ﷺ agreed with the people of the city. It set down in writing what each party could expect of the others. A data contract does the same for a table.

The Sīra also tells of another ṣaḥīfa: the boycott document hung in the Kaaba, found eaten by termites except for the name of Allah. Records decay unless someone looks after them. Sahifa does the looking.
